Concept Description       Concept Description       Concept Description       Concept Description      

The research question guiding this project is: Through painting and field-based observation, the project investigates how personal memory and environmental change intersect within landscapes shaped by climate and human activity.

The landscape is not only a physical place but also a living network of interactions between humans, plants, animals, water, and climate. The exaggerated colors and flowing shapes visualize the emotional and ecological connections embedded in memory and place. By blending abstraction and narrative imagery, the painting aims to explore how personal memory, environmental observation, and ecological imagination can coexist in artistic research about landscape.

Research Methods
  • Fieldwork observation of winter landscapes in northern China
  • Sketching and photographic documentation of rivers, trees, and agricultural fields
  • Memory-based reconstruction of seasonal environmental experience
  • Studio painting translating ecological observations into expressive forms
Reflection

During the painting process, the boundary between human body and natural environment gradually dissolved. This led to an understanding that ecological environments shape psychological experience and identity. The work suggests that humans are embedded within multispecies ecosystems rather than separate from them.

Also to contemplate how environmental conditions such as temperature and radiation influence both physical sensation and emotional perception of urban space. The painting reflects a growing awareness that climate change is not only a scientific issue but also a cultural and psychological experience.
