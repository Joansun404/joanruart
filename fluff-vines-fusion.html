Fluff · Vines · Fusion
Fluff · Vines · Fusion, 2025
Mixed Media

In 2026, my creative focus will be on the environment and the dynamic between humans and non-human beings. My goal is to create a tangible queer ecology through my paintings.

I will delve into the interconnection and harmony between animals, ecology, and humanity, particularly in the face of pressing issues such as climate change, unstable economic systems in urban areas, and the pursuit of symbiotic relationships and emotional understanding.
